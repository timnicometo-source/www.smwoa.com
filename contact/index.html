Security Reminder

Watch for impersonation emails.

SMWOA officers and board members will not ask members by email to purchase gift cards or similar items.

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from SMWOA leadership, do not reply, click links, or purchase anything. Use this contact page to verify the message.
