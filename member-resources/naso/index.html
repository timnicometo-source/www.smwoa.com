Membership Overview

NASO group membership information.

NASO group membership may be available for SMWOA members. MSHSL insurance is provided through dues paid to the MSHSL, but NASO provides more than additional insurance coverage.

NASO offers professional resources, publications, discounts, and support for officials. A link to NASO’s website is provided so members can learn more about current benefits and services.

Group Rate Note

If you have interest in NASO membership, the group rate has previously been listed at $91 for the year, but that amount is subject to change.

NASO membership may only be offered once per year, so it may not always be available during the current payment window.

Benefits

Benefits of NASO membership.

NASO membership can provide additional support for officials through insurance, discounts, publications, and professional development resources.

Insurance

Additional coverage

  • Liability insurance up to $6,000,000.
  • Coverage may apply across all sports for a full 12 months, not only MSHSL contests.
  • Coverage may also apply to non-school or summer officiating opportunities.
Discounts

Member savings

  • Hotel discounts, including Marriott offers.
  • Discounts from selected officiating gear vendors.
  • Auto rental discounts from participating providers.
  • Publication discounts from NASO and Referee resources.
Support

Other benefits

  • Referee Magazine access as part of membership.
  • Professional development resources for officials.
  • Officiating articles, guidance, and member resources.
  • Membership fees may be tax deductible. Members should consult a tax advisor.

Professional Development

More than insurance.

NASO can be a helpful resource for officials who want additional professional support, education, publications, and national officiating information.

Officials should review the current NASO benefits directly before joining, since benefits, rates, and services may change over time.
