Professional Appearance

Step onto the mat ready to officiate.

You are representing both yourself and SMWOA when you step onto the mat, and you only have one chance to make a first impression. Make it a good one.

Officials should make sure their uniform fits properly, is clean, matches their partner when required, and meets MSHSL expectations for varsity contests.

New officials should plan ahead.

Do not wait until your first assignment to check your uniform and equipment. Order early, try everything on, and make sure you have every required item.

Required Gear

Standard equipment for wrestling officials.

The standard equipment required for wrestling officials per MSHSL expectations includes the following items.

01

Officials shirt

Smitty MSHSL gray shirt with a black panel and black collar and sleeve cuffs, with the MSHSL logo on the left chest. Officials must match.

02

Pants and belt

Black pants and a black belt are required. Make sure pants are clean, professional, and appropriate for officiating movement.

03

Shoes and socks

Black shoes are required. White or gray trim is permitted. Black socks should also be worn.

04

Match equipment

Required items include a colored disk, black lanyard, black whistle, and red and green armbands.

New Officials

Before your first match.

New officials should verify their uniform and equipment before accepting their first assignment. Try everything on, make sure your whistle and lanyard are ready, and keep your red and green bands in your bag.

If you are unsure whether an item meets expectations, ask an experienced official, mentor, or association leader before match day.
