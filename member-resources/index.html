Resource Guide

Where should I start?

Different resources are useful at different points in the season. These categories help officials find the right item faster.

Before the Season

Registration, eligibility, and preparation.

  • Check your eligibility status.
  • Review MSHSL official information.
  • Confirm ArbiterSports access.
  • Make sure your uniform and equipment are ready.

During the Season

Assignments, procedures, and professionalism.

  • Use Arbiter for assignments and schedule information.
  • Review the process for blood-related situations.
  • Follow the Code of Ethics.
  • Communicate clearly with schools, coaches, partners, and the association.

Professional Support

Insurance, development, and officiating support.

  • Review NASO membership information.
  • Understand association expectations.
  • Use resources to continue developing as an official.
  • Ask questions when something is unclear.
