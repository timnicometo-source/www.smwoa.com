Become an Official

How to become an SMWOA wrestling official.

The Southern Minnesota Wrestling Officials Association is actively recruiting new members for the upcoming season. The path to becoming an official involves a multi-step process that requires timely completion.

Step 1

Register with the Minnesota State High School League

All officials must be registered with the MSHSL to be eligible to officiate. Please be advised that the MSHSL registration is a detailed process that may take several days to finalize.

Registration Notes

  • MSHSL Information: Review the full MSHSL registration process using the official MSHSL information link.
  • Instructional Video: A video guide is available to help walk you through the registration process.
  • Registration Deadline: Registration for closes approximately one week prior to the season start date.

Please follow the instructions below based on your status as an official.

First-Time Registration

For brand-new officials

If you have never officiated an MSHSL sport, you must create a new account.

  1. Navigate to the MSHSL Arbiter Registration portal.
  2. Follow the on-screen steps to create a new account. If you already have an Arbiter account, simply log in.
  3. Once logged in, either with a new account or an existing one, select the Eligibility button from your dashboard, then click Registration and select the corresponding school year.
  4. Scroll toward the bottom of the page and select Wrestling.
  5. Proceed through the remaining steps to complete your registration as a wrestling official with the MSHSL.

Adding Wrestling

For existing MSHSL officials

If you are already registered with the MSHSL for other sports, you must add wrestling to your officiating profile.

  1. Log in to your MSHSL Arbiter account.
  2. Select the Eligibility button from your dashboard, then click Registration and select the corresponding school year.
  3. Scroll toward the bottom of the page and select Wrestling.
  4. Proceed through the remaining steps to complete your registration as a wrestling official with the MSHSL.

Annual Renewal

MSHSL registration must be renewed annually to remain eligible to officiate wrestling matches. Look for an email from the MSHSL with instructions for renewing your registration each year.

For technical assistance with the ArbiterSports platform, including login or registration issues, contact Arbiter Support directly at 800-311-4060.

Step 2

Attend the SMWOA preseason meeting

The SMWOA preseason meeting for wrestling officials typically occurs in early November. At this meeting, you will be assigned a mentor and receive essential training on scheduling software, officiating fundamentals, and association procedures.

What to expect

New officials will receive important information about ArbiterSports, association expectations, mechanics, communication, and next steps.
