Southern Minnesota Wrestling Officials Association Fees

All SMWOA officials are independent contractors. Please contract directly with each official assigned to work your wrestling event.

Updated 11/12/2024

Varsity Events

Varsity event fee schedule.

The fees below are listed per event type. Mileage is added separately for each official as outlined below.

Varsity Event Type Fee
Dual Meet $130
Dual Head & Assist $260 total
Triangular $210 / $110
Triangular, One Official $260
Quadrangular, Two Varsity Officials $210 each official
Tournament $240 each official

Weigh-in and skin checks are included in the fees above.

Under Varsity Events

Fee schedule by event duration.

For junior varsity, junior high, and youth events, fees are based on the total duration of the tournament or event.

Total Duration Fee
0–4 hours $110.00 per official
4–6 hours $160.00 per official
6–8 hours / 1 day $210.00

Mileage

Mileage calculated one way to the event.

Mileage is added based on the one-way distance to the event location. Schools should confirm mileage and payment expectations directly with assigned officials.

0–15 miles $10.00
16–50 miles $20.00
51–75 miles $40.00
76+ miles $60.00

For Schools

Please work directly with assigned officials.

SMWOA officials are independent contractors. Schools should communicate directly with the assigned official or officials regarding event details, expectations, payment, and any site-specific information.
